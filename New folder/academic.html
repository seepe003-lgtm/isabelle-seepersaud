Academic Background

My educational journey, coursework, and how my studies have shaped my approach to business and leadership.

My Business & Academic Background

I am a Marketing B.B.A. student at the University of Minnesota Duluth's Labovitz School of Business & Economics, and my coursework has played a major role in shaping how I think about business, leadership, and people. As someone interested in marketing and professional leadership, I value understanding not just what businesses do, but why they do it and how their decisions impact consumers, employees, and communities.

Understanding Markets & the Economy

ECON 1023 – Principles of Economics: Micro

I learned how individuals and businesses make decisions when resources are limited. This course introduced me to supply and demand, market competition, pricing strategies, and consumer choice. It helped me understand the psychology behind purchasing decisions and how small changes in price or incentives can significantly impact consumer behavior — knowledge that directly supports my interest in marketing strategy.

ECON 1022 – Principles of Economics: Macro

I studied inflation, unemployment, fiscal and monetary policy, and economic cycles. This course helped me see how broader economic conditions influence consumer confidence and business growth. It taught me to think beyond individual transactions and consider how national and global trends affect marketing, spending, and long-term planning.

Financial Literacy & Decision-Making

ACCT 2001 – Principles of Financial Accounting

This course gave me essential financial literacy skills. I learned how to read and interpret financial statements, understand revenue and expenses, and evaluate a company's financial health. This experience helped me realize how important financial awareness is for making responsible business decisions.

ACCT 2002 – Principles of Managerial Accounting

This course focused on budgeting, cost analysis, and internal decision-making. It showed me how managers use financial data to evaluate performance and plan strategically. From a marketing perspective, it taught me to think carefully about budgets, efficiency, and return on investment.

Law, Ethics & Social Awareness in Business

BLAW 2001 – Legal Environment of Business

I learned how laws shape everyday business practices, including contracts, employment law, torts, and regulatory compliance. This course helped me understand the legal responsibilities businesses have and how to avoid risk while operating ethically and professionally.

BLAW 1001 – Race and the Law

I explored how legal systems intersect with race and social inequality. This course challenged me to think critically about ethics, inclusion, and responsibility. It strongly influenced how I view leadership and branding, reinforcing that successful businesses must be socially aware and intentional about the impact they have on diverse communities.

Data, Technology & Analytical Thinking

ECON 2030 – Applied Statistics

I developed skills in data analysis, probability, and interpreting results. This course strengthened my ability to evaluate information objectively and make data-driven decisions. It directly supports my interest in marketing analytics and understanding how performance, trends, and outcomes can be measured.

MIS 2201 – IT in Business

I gained technical insight into how information systems support business operations and decision-making. This course helped me understand how technology, data systems, and digital tools are integrated into modern organizations, which is especially relevant in today's marketing and customer-focused environments.

Communication, Teamwork & Professional Growth

LSBE 2000 – Interpersonal & Teamwork Skills

This course focused on communication, collaboration, and conflict resolution in professional settings. It strengthened my ability to work effectively with others and reinforced leadership skills that I actively use in my role as a Residential Advisor.

LSBE 3000 – Career Development

This course is helping me refine my professional identity and engage in intentional career planning.

WRIT 3121 – Advanced Writing: Business & Organizations

This course emphasizes clear communication and professional writing, allowing me to present ideas confidently and communicate in a polished, business-appropriate way.
